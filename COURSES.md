# Courses and mechanics

SkyBound Showdown has **six playable courses**, generated by `scripts/main.gd`. Each course places its platforms, coins, finish flag, and two-button switch puzzle along a different route. Course length and obstacle complexity increase as you progress; the sixth course, Gimmick Gauntlet, introduces a full set of movement-surface and hazard gimmicks.

Press **Q** to shift between Material and Mirror dimensions. Mirror platforms sit on an offset route, and selected obstacles appear in only one dimension.

| # | Course | Route size | Main challenges |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Cloud Hop** | 15 platforms | Gentle climb, sky hoops, rotating sweepers, falling platforms, and an anti-gravity zone |
| 2 | **Switchback Sprint** | 20 platforms | Moving platforms, jump hurdles, rotating sweepers, falling platforms, and an anti-gravity zone |
| 3 | **Moonflip Maze** | 25 platforms | Maze walls, timed phase platforms, rotating sweepers, and falling platforms |
| 4 | **Rising Ruins** | 30 platforms | Climbing route, maze walls, moving and timed platforms, rotating sweepers, falling platforms, and an anti-gravity zone |
| 5 | **Sky Crown** | 36 platforms | The longest of the first five courses, with golden gates, crown spires, more sweepers, and an anti-gravity zone |
| 6 | **Gimmick Gauntlet** | 42 platforms | See-saws, rotating logs, slippery and sticky zones, bounce pads, fan tunnels, a slide, rolling barrels, moving and timed platforms, hurdles, and maze walls |

## Shared course loop

1. Explore both routes and collect every coin.
2. Shift dimensions with **Q** to use Material and Mirror platforms.
3. Find and activate both switches with **E** to open the gates along the route.
4. Reach the finish flag to complete the course.

Falling platforms collapse when the player lands on them. Timed phase platforms switch between active and inactive states, so time your jumps. Rotating sweepers block the Mirror path and must be timed or jumped. Maze walls require players to follow the openings or shift dimensions; sprint hurdles can be jumped over. Anti-gravity zones appear on selected courses and reverse the player's gravity while they are inside. Press **F** to slow the game briefly; the ability recharges after a cooldown.

Each course has two checkpoint beacons along its route. Touch a beacon to save your respawn point; falling after that returns you to the latest beacon without restarting the course.

## Gimmick Gauntlet: course 6

Gimmick Gauntlet is the most mechanically varied course. Its 42-platform route combines the shared dimension-shifting and switch-gate puzzle with several extra hazards and surfaces:

- **See-saws** tilt as you move across them.
- **Rotating logs** make narrow crossings harder to time.
- **Slippery zones and a slide** reduce traction and challenge your control.
- **Sticky zones** change the feel of movement.
- **Bounce pads** launch you upward.
- **Fan tunnels** lift the player through vertical wind columns.
- **Rolling barrels** move along the route and must be avoided.
- **Moving and timed platforms**, plus the usual falling platforms, require careful timing.

## Scenic markers

The glowing upright hoops are decorative route markers. They have no collision and do not block or boost the player.

## Level progression

The home screen allows direct access to any of the six courses. Completing a course offers the next one; completing Gimmick Gauntlet cycles back to Cloud Hop for another run.
